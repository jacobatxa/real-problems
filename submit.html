提交一个真问题Submit a real problem

大问题、小问题都欢迎。每一条都会由导师按五个维度打分，10 分以上的会出现在问题墙上。 Big or small, every problem is welcome. A mentor scores each one on five dimensions, and 10+ goes on the wall.

怎样写好一个问题How to describe a problem

  1. 说清楚谁在受困扰Say who is affected 是同学、爷爷奶奶，还是某个地方的人？Classmates, grandparents, or people in a certain place?
  2. 讲一个具体的场景Describe a real moment 什么时候、在哪里，发生了什么。When and where it happens, and what goes wrong.
  3. AI 的想法可以留空AI ideas are optional 有想法就写，没有也没关系，我们一起想。Share one if you have it. If not, we will think it through together.
例子EXAMPLE
我奶奶看不清药盒上的小字，常常记不住哪顿该吃哪种药。家里有很多老人都有同样的烦恼。 My grandma cannot read the small print on her medicine boxes and often forgets which pill to take when. Many older people have the same trouble.

未满 14 岁的同学，请和爸爸妈妈一起填写。不要填写身份证号、家庭住址等个人信息。 Under 14? Please fill this in with a parent. Do not include ID numbers, home addresses, or other personal details.
