怎么挑选How we choose

每个问题，都用同一把尺子来量Every problem is measured with the same ruler

收到的问题五花八门，所以我们用五个维度给每一条打分，分数够了才会上墙，更高的才会变成项目。Submissions vary a lot, so we score each one on five dimensions. Enough points puts it on the wall; more makes it a project.

01

收到问题A problem arrives

来自提交表单，或我们从全球公开报告里整理。From the submission form, or gathered from public global reports.

02

先过安全底线Safety check first

不涉及隐私、危险和医疗诊断，孩子才能参与。No private data, danger, or medical diagnosis, so young people can take part.

03

导师按五个维度打分Mentors score five dimensions

每项 1 到 3 分，满分 15 分，并补上可靠来源。1 to 3 points each, 15 in total, plus a trusted source.

04

上墙，或成为项目On the wall, or a project

10 分以上上墙；12 分以上并找到导师，就开始组队。10+ goes on the wall. 12+ with a mentor becomes a team project.

成为项目后，团队会来采访你，做出第一版请你试用。Once it is a project, the team will interview you and ask you to try the first version.
